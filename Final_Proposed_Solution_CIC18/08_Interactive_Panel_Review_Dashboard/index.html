Viva Cheat Sheet: Concise Examiner Q&A
Q: Why does traditional IDS fail on Zero-Day attacks?
Traditional classifiers use Softmax at their output. Because Softmax is constrained to sum to 100%, when an unobserved alien attack appears, the model is forced to assign high probability to one of the known classes (the Softmax Overconfidence Fallacy).
Q: How does Free Energy solve the Softmax Overconfidence Fallacy?
Free Energy $E(x) = -T \cdot \log \sum \exp(f_k(x) / T)$ operates on raw unnormalized logits. For known classes, logit energy is low; for alien zero-day threats, logits across all classes are suppressed, yielding an unmistakably high Free Energy novelty score.
Q: How did you handle the extreme 16.2M flow class imbalance in CSE-CIC-IDS2018?
We used Equal-Quota Streaming Stratified Reservoir Sampling in 150k chunks: 100% of all rare minority classes (SQL Injection: 87, XSS: 230, Web BF: 611, LOIC-UDP: 1,730) were preserved, while high-volume floods were capped at 5,000 flows each. Peak RAM was <1.5 GB.
Q: How do you prove there is Zero Data Leakage?
We stripped IP addresses, timestamps, and sequence numbers. Every flow was assigned an immutable integer row_id, and we asserted $|Train \cap Test| == 0$ with zero synthetic sample contamination in the test partition.
