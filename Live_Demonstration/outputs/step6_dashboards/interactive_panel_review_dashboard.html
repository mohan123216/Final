Mathematical Formulation of the 4 Foundational Signals Tier 2 Mathematics
Signal 1: Logit Free Energy Novelty (S_E) Fusion Weight: 0.40
S_E(x) = -T · ln ( ∑_{k=1}^K exp( f_k(x) / T ) )

The Problem Solved: Eliminates the Softmax 100%-sum fallacy. Softmax mathematically forces probabilities to sum to 1.0, creating false confidence on novel attacks.

Why Free Energy Works: Operates directly on raw unconstrained tree margin logits output_margin=True. Known classes produce winning logits ($f_{\text{win}} \gg 0$) giving high partition functions and large negative free energy ($S_E \ll 0$). Novel zero-days have flat/depressed logits, producing significantly higher Free Energy ($S_E \gg 0$).

Signal 2: Log-Manifold Ledoit-Wolf Mahalanobis (S_M) Fusion Weight: 0.30
S_M(x) = √[ (z - μ_c)^T · Σ_c⁻¹ · (z - μ_c) ]

The Problem Solved: Prevents covariance smearing and singular covariance matrices on heavy-tailed packet volume counts (e.g. bytes, dur, rate).

Why It Works: Applies log1p transformation $\log(1 + x)$ followed by Ledoit-Wolf regularized shrinkage estimation: $\Sigma_{\text{LW}} = (1 - \delta) \Sigma + \delta \mu I$. Ensures non-singular precision matrices and accurate geometric separation in metric space.

Signal 3: Tree Leaf-Space Traversal Novelty (S_L) Fusion Weight: 0.15
S_L(x) = 1.0 - (1 / T_trees) ∑_{t=1}^{T_trees} Freq_t(leaf_t(x) | c_pred)

The Problem Solved: Captures decision tree structural path anomalies that continuous Euclidean/Mahalanobis geometry misses.

Why It Works: We profile the leaf IDs traversed across all 100 XGBoost trees during training. A novel attack may mimic certain packet counters, but its multivariate feature interactions force it into unprecedented leaf nodes never reached by known training traffic.

Signal 4: Relative Distance Margin (S_R) Fusion Weight: 0.15
S_R(x) = D_M(x, c_pred) / [ min_{j ≠ c_pred} D_M(x, j) + ε ]

The Problem Solved: Resolves centroid ambiguity when an alien zero-day flow falls into the void equidistant between two known clusters.

Why It Works: For genuine known flows, the distance to the true class is far smaller than the distance to any other class ($S_R \approx 0.1 - 0.3$). For borderline zero-day flows sitting in interstitial voids, $D_M(\text{pred}) \approx D_M(\text{other})$, causing $S_R \rightarrow 1.0$, exposing the boundary violation.

Empirical CDF Rank Calibration & Threshold Selection (τ) Calibration Protocol

Why Empirical CDF Normalization?

The 4 signals have vastly different mathematical domains:

  • Free Energy $S_E \in (-\infty, +\infty)$ (e.g. -15.0 to +4.0)
  • Mahalanobis Distance $S_M \in [0, +\infty)$ (e.g. 0.5 to 25.0)
  • Leaf Space Novelty $S_L \in [0.0, 1.0]$
  • Relative Distance Ratio $S_R \in [0.0, +\infty)$ (e.g. 0.1 to 3.5)

Simple linear min-max scaling fails because heavy outliers compress in-distribution points. Gaussian z-score scaling fails because distributions are non-Gaussian and skewed.

Our Solution: Non-Parametric Empirical CDF Calibration:
We fit empirical CDFs strictly on the 9,152 Validation Known Flows. Using np.searchsorted, every raw signal is transformed into its exact percentile rank $Z_i \in [0.0, 1.0]$.

Z_i(s) = F_i(s) = P(S_known ≤ s) = rank(s) / N_val

Calibrating the Operational Threshold (τ)

The operating threshold $\tau$ is chosen strictly from known validation traffic:

τ = np.percentile(S_val_unified, 95.0) = 0.8407
Guarantees of the 95th Percentile Cutoff:
✓ 95.19% Known Traffic Acceptance: Exactly matches the calibrated 95% budget on unseen test data.
✓ 97.34% Benign Normal Specificity: Normal false alarms kept to only 2.66% (under 3% in all trials).
✓ Zero Heuristics: Does not rely on brittle hardcoded port rules or protocol filters. Pure data-driven statistical boundary.

Panel Review & Viva Defense Rapid Response Cards Oral Examination Guide
Q1 Why did you sample 61,130 flows instead of training on all 73.3 million flows?
The raw 73.3M Bot-IoT dataset is 99.9% DoS/DDoS floods (71.4M flows) and only 0.013% Normal traffic (9,543 flows). Training on all 73.3M flows creates catastrophic majority-class bias where a naive model predicting DDoS achieves 99.9% accuracy while failing completely on real threats. Our streaming equal-quota reservoir retained 100% of all 9,543 Normal flows, 100% of all 1,587 Theft flows, and capped flood subclasses at 10,000 each, preserving the full information spectrum in 3.7 MB.
Q2 What is the Softmax Overconfidence Fallacy, and why does naive confidence (1 - P_max) fail?
Softmax forces all probabilities to sum to 100%: $\sum P_k = 1.0$. When an unseen zero-day attack arrives, the model has no "unknown" node; it is mathematically forced to arbitrarily inflate one of the known classes with high false certainty ($P_{\max} \approx 0.99 \implies 1 - P_{\max} \approx 0.01$). This masks novelty and collapses zero-day recall to 33.3%. We replaced it with Logit Free Energy Novelty ($S_E$), which operates on unconstrained booster margins ($f_k(x)$) where unseen attacks exhibit distinctively high energy.
Q3 How did you fix the Data Exfiltration Precision collapse (0.08 in base paper vs 0.97 in yours)?
The base paper suffered from the Base Rate Fallacy. Because Data Exfiltration has only 118 flows, even a 0.5% false alarm rate on normal traffic creates dozens of false positives that overwhelm the 118 true positives, driving precision down to 0.08. We engineered Directional Flow Asymmetry features (sbytes/dbytes and spkts/dpkts). Legitimate IoT sensor telemetry has bidirectional symmetric ping-ack handshakes; data exfiltration exhibits massive unidirectional source-to-destination payload asymmetry. This eliminated false alarms and elevated Precision to 0.97 and F1 to 0.95.
Q4 How do you prove mathematically that your results are not caused by data leakage?
We enforced 3 cryptographic integrity controls: 1. Feature Stripping: Removed all source/destination IPs (saddr, daddr), MACs, and timestamps to prevent memorizing testbed host artifacts. 2. Row-ID Intersection Assertions: Every flow has a unique row ID; our code strictly asserts $\text{Train IDs} \cap \text{Test IDs} = \emptyset$. 3. Zero Test Contamination: No synthetic oversampling (SMOTE) or threshold tuning was ever performed on the test data.
